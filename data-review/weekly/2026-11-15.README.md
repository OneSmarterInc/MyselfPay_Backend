## Evidence rules and limitations

Only official provider sources and provider-linked authorized pricing marketplaces were used for price findings. A price is kept with its exact modality, body-part/protocol, contrast wording, reading/facility scope, eligibility, payment pathway and location limitations. Starting prices, ranges, estimates, averages, maxima and promotions are not converted to fixed all-in cash quotes. Additional menu entries are not presumed newly introduced.

`Source observation date` means the date this audit reviewed the material. It is not a rate-effective date and does not imply a same-day origin fetch. Web results may be cached; crawl-age and retrieval limitations are recorded per source. An inaccessible price page is not proof that the provider removed or changed the price. Older schedules are preserved as dated evidence, not current guarantees. Closed-site historical values remain in historical columns only; observed columns explicitly state no current offer.

The ten network policies establish only public quoting/billing pathways. No price-estimate form, booking, payment, provider outreach or patient data submission occurred. Contact/address details mentioned in a pricing source do not constitute a full directory/contact audit.
